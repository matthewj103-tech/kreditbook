Privacy policy

Kredit Book

By DOYC (Pty) Ltd · Last updated 21 October 2026

In short

Kredit Book is a record-keeping app for tracking money people owe you. Everything you enter stays on your phone. DOYC does not have a server for the app, does not collect your book, and cannot see it.

Who we are

Kredit Book is made by DOYC (Pty) Ltd, South Africa (doyc.co.za). If you have a question about this policy or your information, email matthew@doyc.co.za.

What the app stores, and where

You type in the information yourself. It is saved only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • names and cellphone numbers of the people in your book
  • amounts, interest, dates, repayments and notes
  • optional collection details you choose to add, such as a payday or where to find someone
  • your settings, such as your book name, your name, payment details and NCR number

Your PIN is never stored. The app keeps only a scrambled (hashed) version of it in your phone's secure storage, together with a hashed version of your recovery code.

The app has no user accounts and does not send your book to DOYC or anyone else.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to do it:

  • WhatsApp messages. When you tap a reminder, statement or receipt button, the app opens WhatsApp with the message typed in. You decide whether to send it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Backups. When you tap "Make a backup", the app creates a file of your book and lets you choose where to send or save it, such as WhatsApp or Google Drive. Keep backups somewhere private, because anyone with the file can read your book.

Payments

Basic and Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Google Play. Google handles the payment. DOYC never receives your card or bank details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock the right features. Google's privacy policy applies to your payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • no advertising and no ad tracking
  • no analytics or usage tracking
  • no selling or sharing of information
  • no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

The app uses the internet only to check your subscription with Google Play and to open links such as WhatsApp.

Information about other people

The people in your book have not given their details to DOYC; you have entered them. You are responsible for using their information fairly and lawfully, including under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Only record what you need, keep your phone and backups secure, and delete records you no longer need.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a single loan or repayment inside the app.
  • Delete your whole book in Settings → Start fresh → Delete everything.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored by it on your phone.
  • Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted from where you saved them.

Because DOYC holds none of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

The app is protected by your PIN and locks itself after two minutes in the background. In the released app, screenshots of the app are blocked to protect your book. Keep your phone locked and your recovery code private.

Children

Kredit Book is meant for adults running their own records. It is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
